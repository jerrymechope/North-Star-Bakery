Our Story

Founded with a passion for traditional craftsmanship, North Star Bakery has been a cornerstone of the community, bringing neighbors together over the warm, inviting scent of freshly milled grains and slow-risen doughs.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that real bread takes time. By utilizing wild fermentation, unbleached local flours, and traditional hand-laminated techniques, we honor the ancient art of baking while serving our local neighborhood.

Staff Highlights

Head Baker & Founder: Elena Vance leads our pre-dawn bake rotation, bringing over fifteen years of wild-yeast sourdough expertise to our deck ovens.

Pastry Chef: Marcus Thorne oversees our morning lamination station, hand-rolling butter layers every single day for our signature croissants and danishes.
